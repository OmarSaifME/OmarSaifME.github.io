Bio-inspired CFD

Numerical investigation of bio-inspired wing planforms as fixed wings at cruise speed for commercial aircrafts (ongoing study)

Background & Overview

The ongoing numerical investigation of bio-inspired wings (as fixed wings) in their gliding form is informed by my undergraduate thesis (linked below). In this study, I am extending the scope of the prior study and focusing on cruise speed with updated numerical model and fluid domain. In addition to these updates, the study will entertain more rigorous methodology (compared to the prior study) and geometry preparation. The model choice has been confirmed already with a validated set of data (based on publication of ONERA M6 wing), and a three-mesh Grid Convergence Index. The study is currently in the data production phase. Any updates on the study (manuscript submission, acceptance) will be available in this page as soon as they happen.
