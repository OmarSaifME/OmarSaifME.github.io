AoA Calculation Script

A python calculation script for derived values of key settings to manipulate freestream flow based on Angle of Attack input for a 2D CFD simulation in ANSYS Fluent.

Background & Overview

There are two ways to manipulate freestream flow (based on Angle of Attack) in an aerodynamic CFD study. The first, and obvious one would be to rotate the model geometry. However, such practice will require new mesh generation that hinders the speed and efficiency of such analysis, specifically if the practitioner intends to do a parametric sweep (multiple AoA for example). This also adds complexity in devising quantification error studies like the Grid Convergence Index (Celik et al., 2008). Therefore, although straightforward, such process is not desirable for sustainable and repetitive simulation runs. On the other hand, by keeping the model fixed and using equation derived data in the inlet settings, manipulating freestream flow respecting the necessary AoA for the study leads to only a few numbers being changed - facilitating repeatable workflow for reducing complexity and improving accuracy.

For a 2D CFD study, there are a few settings (a total of 6 values to be exact) one may change based on the Angle of Attack in question. These settings are as follows - ● Inlet Settings: Velocity component along X and Y axis
● Drag Force Vectors: Force vector component for drag in report definition
● Lift Force Vectors: Force vector component for lift in report definition

Just by changing 6 numbers (2 for each of the aforementioned module), one may achieve the necessary 'angled' freestream, alongside force reports typically necessary for such aerodynamic analysis. The numbers are derived from trigonometric functions associated with vectors. Excusing my crude penmanship, one may find more about the equations in the picture below.
