Product Design: Mac Mini

Apple Mac Mini: Photorealistic Rendering

A self-assigned exercise in reverse-engineering consumer electronics. The Mac Mini's seemingly simple geometry demands precise fillets, draft angles, and gap tolerances to look convincing. Rendered in SolidWorks Visualize to test how well SolidWorks handles reflective aluminum and use of decals (apple logo) natively.

Design Tool: SolidWorks
Render Tool: SolidWorks Visualize
